## Poyshakori

Take Control of Your Money.
Poyshakori is a personal finance and money management application designed to help individuals track their income, manage expenses, organize financial accounts, and gain better control over their money.

Built with a focus on simplicity, privacy, and financial clarity, Poyshakori aims to make everyday money management easier through an intuitive and organized experience.

Key Features

- Personal Finance Management — Keep your financial records organized in one place.
- Income Tracking — Record and review income from different sources.
- Expense Tracking — Monitor spending and understand where your money goes.
- Account Management — Organize cash, bank accounts, and mobile wallets.
- Transaction History — Search, filter, and review financial activities.
- Budget Planning — Set spending limits and monitor your budget.
- Debt Management — Track money borrowed, lent, and repaid.
- Savings Goals — Set financial goals and monitor your progress.
- Financial Reports — Review income, expenses, and spending patterns.
- Offline-First Experience — Designed to support core financial tasks without an internet connection.
- Privacy-Focused Design — Built with an emphasis on keeping personal financial information under the user's control.
- Backup and Restore — Planned support for secure, user-controlled data backups.

Technology

- Kotlin
- Android
- Jetpack Compose
- MVVM Architecture
- Room Database (SQLite)

Our Vision

The goal of Poyshakori is to provide a practical, reliable, and privacy-conscious way to manage personal finances without unnecessary complexity.

From everyday expenses to long-term savings, Poyshakori aims to help users make more informed financial decisions.

Development Status
Poyshakori is currently under development. Features and implementation details may evolve as the application progresses toward release.
